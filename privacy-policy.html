FlipFrame Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how the FlipFrame app for Android ("FlipFrame", "the app") handles your information. FlipFrame is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

The short version: FlipFrame does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your videos are edited on your device and never leave it.

Information we collect

None. FlipFrame has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

Your videos

  • FlipFrame only sees the videos you choose to open, either through the Android photo picker or by sharing a video to the app. It does not ask for access to your whole gallery.
  • All editing, subject tracking and exporting happen on your device.
  • While exporting, the app writes a temporary file to its private storage on your phone. This file is replaced by the next export and is removed if you clear the app's cache or uninstall the app.
  • The finished video is saved only where you choose. If you tap Share, the video goes only to the app you pick in the Android share menu.

Permissions

FlipFrame does not ask for any special permissions. Its video library includes two standard Android permissions that do not involve personal data: one that keeps the device awake while a video is processing, and one that lets the library check whether the device is online. FlipFrame does not use the internet.

Google Play

When you download FlipFrame, Google Play processes data under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google, other than the anonymous, combined statistics (such as install counts) that Google shows to all app developers.

Children

FlipFrame is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how FlipFrame handles information (for example, if a future version adds new features), we will update this page and change the effective date above. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
